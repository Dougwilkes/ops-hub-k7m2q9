The mechanism — why nobody caught it

Doug's master statement tracker dates it precisely: reconciliation stopped after 2/29/2024 on VROI and July 2024 on VRE, and TD HELOC statements read “Requested” every month of 2023 and 2024 and were never received. The account-type flips and drifting balances all happened inside a window where nothing was being tied to a statement. The fix is not four corrections — it is restarting monthly reconciliation.

Working in this system

the weekly rhythm
  1. Monday: the pipeline files fresh QBO reports for every entity into the Drive folder automatically.
  2. Dave works items from the Assignments & Notes tab (Phase 1 first), makes the entries in QBO, and marks the row DONE with a note.
  3. Doug + Claude verify DONE items against the next Monday's fresh reports — corrections show up in the data within a week, so nothing stays "done" on trust alone.
  4. David resolves the tax-treatment questions routed to him on the same tab; his rulings get added to the Settled Rulings list so they're never re-argued.
  5. Anyone with a new question or discovery adds a row to Assignments & Notes — that tab is the single queue for the whole engagement.
Ground rules: 2025 is not closed — book corrections into 2025 freely. Don't rename accounts from the COA Mapping tab yet; it's a proposal awaiting David's review. When a bank feed shows “Terra Notes,” it's the land note. When in doubt, ask on the Assignments tab rather than guessing — every settled ruling above started as a question.
